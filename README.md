# Commutation.app

**[Commutation.app](https://commutation.app)** helps attorneys, and the incarcerated Oklahomans and families they serve, prepare commutation and clemency applications, including the reentry plan.

The app guides attorneys and applicants through the process, organizes public court and corrections records, and uses AI to help draft clear, well-supported applications. A Professional plan gives attorneys and legal-aid teams AI drafting and research tools.

## About
